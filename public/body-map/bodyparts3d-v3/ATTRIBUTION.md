## Independently authored anatomy

The bilateral latissimus dorsi, rectus abdominis, internal oblique, and transversus abdominis volumes are BodyCast-authored educational visual approximations, built from documented attachments and muscle-layer/fiber directions. They are not copied or renamed BodyParts3D meshes and are not covered by the BodyParts3D attribution above. They require anatomy review before being treated as authoritative.
